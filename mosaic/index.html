How to play

One rule across every geometry

Each clue is an ordered list of colored runs. [Yellow 2] [Blue 3] means two connected yellow cells followed later—or immediately, because the colors differ—by three connected blue cells. Runs of the same color require at least one empty cell between them.

Two picture types

Color picture keeps different colors in the clue runs. Black & white picture converts every non-empty picture block into one filled block and uses standard number clues.

Time is a real clue direction

In +Time modes, spatial clues apply inside each frame. Selecting one cell also reveals its ordered run through all frames. The timeline is therefore part of the logic, not a countdown.

Three complementary 3D views

Model gives context, Slice exposes internal layers, and Track isolates exactly one clue line. Use Draw, Empty, or Clean for editing; use Move / Orbit when you want camera control.
